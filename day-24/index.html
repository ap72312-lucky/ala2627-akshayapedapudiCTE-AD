What am I learning?

Last week you got flexbox working on a practice box. Today it goes on your own page, and you make it do what you actually want instead of what it happens to do.

One warning before you start. Almost everyone learns justify-content and align-items as 'across' and 'down' and it works fine — right up until flex-direction changes to column, and then both of them do the opposite of what you remember. They are not across and down. They are along the main axis and across it, and flex-direction decides which is which. If you learn it that way today you never have to unlearn it.
