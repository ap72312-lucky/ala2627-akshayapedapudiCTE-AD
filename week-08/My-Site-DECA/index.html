What is DECA?

DECA, an abbreviation of Distributive Education Clubs of America, is a global student organization that prepares high school and college students for careers in business & management, marketing, finance, and hospitality

What DECA does.

DECA connects students with real-world business experiences and opportunities. Students are able to compete in various events and conferences, gaining valuable skills and knowledge. Containing scholarships and internships, these are valuable assets to aspiring leaders.
